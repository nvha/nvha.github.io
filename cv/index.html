Professional Summary

Hardware Systems Architect and Senior Network Systems Architect (Ph.D.) with 3+ years of post-doctoral R&D and 5+ years of university research leadership bridging high-throughput FPGA hardware acceleration with scale-out network protocol design. On the hardware side, brings deep expertise in full-stack RTL design (SystemVerilog/Verilog), high-speed DSP pipelines, ultra-wide 1024-bit/512-bit AXI-Stream datapaths, PCIe 3.0 DMA offload engines, and multi-vendor FPGA prototyping across Intel Agilex and AMD Xilinx architectures. On the networking side, specializes in high-speed transport protocol optimization (TCP/NC, RoCE/RDMA), Software-Defined Networking (SDN/OpenFlow), P4 in-band telemetry, active queue management (AQM), and discrete-event simulation in ns-3 and Mininet. Backed by 40+ peer-reviewed publications (IEEE/IEICE) and over $100,000 in R&D grants, excels at hardware-software co-design to translate complex network control logic into low-latency physical silicon and programmable data planes.

    • Engineered a custom 8-byte Control Header featuring a 40-bit Success_Check sequence to track jitter and queue occupancy with microsecond precision.
    • Implemented data-plane packet buffering via a P4 recirculation port queue for localized retransmissions.
