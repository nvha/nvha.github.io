Professional Summary

Principal Network Systems R&D Architect and Hardware Engineer (Ph.D.) with 8+ years of combined post-doctoral R&D and research leadership bridging scale-out network transport protocol design with physical FPGA silicon acceleration. Recognized for a versatile technical profile capable of operating across traditional functional boundaries: leading dedicated software/protocol architecture groups, embedded RTL design teams, or cross-functional HW/SW Co-Design divisions. On the networking side, specializes in low-latency transport protocols (TCP/NC), P4 programmable data planes, active queue management (AQM), dynamic SDN routing, and discrete-event C++/Python network modeling in ns-3. On the hardware side, offers expertise in SystemVerilog RTL, ultra-wide 1024-bit AXI4-Stream datapaths, PCIe 3.0 DMA offload engines, high-speed DSP pipelines, and multi-vendor FPGA emulation across Intel Agilex/Stratix and AMD Xilinx Kintex UltraScale+ platforms. Backed by 40+ peer-reviewed publications (IEEE/IEICE) and $100k+ in competitive grants, excels at transforming complex protocol mathematics into high-performance production hardware and software architectures.
