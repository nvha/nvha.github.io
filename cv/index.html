Professional Summary

Innovative Hardware Systems Architect, Senior Network Systems Architect & R&D Lead (Ph.D.) with 3 years of dedicated post-doctoral R&D alongside 5+ years of university research leadership. Proven expert in translating advanced transport protocols, Software-Defined Networking (SDN/OpenFlow) control planes, and networking logic into high-performance physical-layer silicon and programmable data planes. Proven record of protocol innovation with 40+ peer-reviewed publications across IEEE and IEICE, specializing in TCP/NC frameworks, custom ACK generation mechanics, tail-loss mitigation, active queue management (AQM), dynamic load balancing, and P4 programmable data planes. Expert in full-stack RTL design (SystemVerilog/Verilog), high-speed DSP pipelines, HW/SW co-design, pre-silicon validation, and discrete-event network simulation (ns-3, Mininet) across Intel Agilex, Stratix, and AMD Xilinx architectures.
