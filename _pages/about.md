I am a **Network Systems Architect and Ph.D. Researcher** with 3+ years of postdoctoral R&D experience and 5+ years leading university research initiatives. My work bridges the gap between theoretical transport protocol design, programmable data planes, and high-throughput hardware acceleration.

My research and engineering focus on solving performance bottlenecks in dynamic, lossy, and scale-out network topologies—ranging from high-speed data centers to low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite constellations.

---

### Core Research & Technical Focus

* **Software-Defined Networking & Traffic Engineering:** Architecting self-healing routing architectures, dynamic OpenFlow load-balancing mechanisms (SDN-FEDRP), and fast-reroute algorithms to prevent congestion and eliminate tail latency.
* **P4 In-Band Telemetry & Observability:** Designing P4 data-plane telemetry stacks (BMv2 switch / ns-3) using custom control headers for microsecond-precision jitter, latency, and queue occupancy tracking.
* **Resilient Transport Stacks & Loss Masking:** Engineering Network Coding-assisted transport protocols (TCP/NC), active queue management (AQM), and custom state machines that proactively mask link loss and prevent timeout degradation.
* **Hardware-Software Co-Design on FPGAs:** Designing ultra-wide 1024-bit packet processing pipelines (up to 320 Gbps capacity), PCIe 3.0x4 DMA streaming interfaces, and high-speed transceiver hardware-in-the-loop (HITL) setups across Intel Agilex 5 and Xilinx Kintex UltraScale+ platforms.

---

### Academic & Leadership Background

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Systems Engineering from **Kyushu Institute of Technology** (Japan), where I served as Principal Investigator (PI) for two JSPS KAKEN research grants. Most recently, I co-founded and supervised R&D operations at the **ICTLAB** at the **University of Science** (Vietnam), directing graduate and undergraduate researchers on programmable data planes and transport protocol performance.

I have authored over **40 peer-reviewed international publications** across IEEE, IEICE, and Scopus-indexed venues, and have been awarded 3 international conference Best Paper Awards.
